## Dataset

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| **Source** | NHS England — A&E Attendances & Emergency Admissions |
| **Period** | January 2026 – March 2026 (Q1) |
| **Geography** | England — 42 Integrated Care Boards |
| **Records** | 126 rows (42 ICBs × 3 months) |
| **Access** | [NHS England Statistics](https://www.england.nhs.uk/statistics/statistical-work-areas/ae-waiting-times-and-activity/) |

**Key fields analysed:**
- Total A&E Attendances
- Patients Seen Within 4 Hours
- 4-Hour Compliance Rate (All Departments & Type 1 Major A&E)
- Total Emergency Admissions
- 12-Hour Breach Count

## Key Findings

### Quarter Overview

| Metric | Q1 Total |
|---|---|
| Total A&E Attendances | **6,869,969** |
| Patients Seen Within 4 Hours | **5,124,811** |
| Patients Waiting Over 4 Hours | **1,745,158** |
| Average 4hr Compliance Rate | **73.5%** |
| Average Type 1 (Major A&E) Rate | **59.0%** |

> The NHS 4-hour standard target is **95%** — no ICB in England achieved this in Q1 2026.

---

### Month-on-Month Trend

Performance **improved steadily** across Q1:

| Month | 4hr Rate (All) | 4hr Rate (Type 1) |
|---|---|---|
| January | 71.4% | 56.0% |
| February | 73.0% | 58.1% |
| March | **76.0%** | **62.8%** |

March saw the strongest performance, likely reflecting seasonal demand reduction after winter peaks.

---

### Worst Performing ICBs (4hr Compliance)

| ICB | Jan 4hr Rate | Notable Issue |
|---|---|---|
| NHS Shropshire, Telford & Wrekin | 60.1% | Consistently lowest across all 3 months |
| NHS Nottingham & Nottinghamshire | 64.0% | High 12hr breaches |
| NHS Herefordshire & Worcestershire | 63.4% | Below 70% for 2 of 3 months |

---

### Best Performing ICBs (4hr Compliance)

| ICB | Mar 4hr Rate |
|---|---|
| NHS North West London | 83.5% |
| NHS Kent & Medway | 81.6% |
| NHS Hertfordshire & West Essex | 82.6% |

### Highest 12-Hour Breach Volumes (Q1 Total)

| ICB | Q1 12hr Breaches |
|---|---|
